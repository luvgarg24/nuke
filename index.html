Why NUKE

“System Data” isn't an answer.

macOS tells you storage is full, but not what is actually disposable. NUKE turns filesystem paths into plain decisions: what it is, how much space it uses, and what happens if you remove it.

01

Scan.

One click scans known cache and app-data locations. No Full Disk Access wall.

02

Understand.

See the size, exact source and consequence before anything is removed.

03

Nuke.

Known disposable data is pre-selected. Uncertain large caches stay in Review for your call.

Privacy

Your files stay yours.

NUKE scans on your Mac. There is no cloud scanner, account, analytics SDK or telemetry. The current alpha does not ask for Full Disk Access.
