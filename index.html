Why NUKE

“System Data” isn't an answer.

macOS tells you storage is full, but not what is actually disposable. NUKE turns filesystem paths into plain decisions: what it is, how much space it uses, and what happens if you remove it.

01

Scan.

One click scans known cache and app-data locations.

02

Understand.

NUKE tells you the real story behind what's using your storage.

03

Nuke.

Regenerable data is ready to nuke. Anything uncertain stays your call.

Privacy

Your files stay yours.

NUKE scans on your Mac. There is no cloud scanner, account, analytics SDK or telemetry.
