THE PROBLEM

“System Data”
isn't an answer.

macOS tells you you're out of space, then hides tens of gigabytes behind vague categories. NUKE translates filesystem garbage into decisions a human can actually make.

01

Scan.

NUKE maps the storage it can access and identifies known offenders. No permission wall before you've even seen the app.

02

Understand.

Every finding tells you what it is, why it's there, its exact path, and what happens if you remove it.

03

Nuke.

Known regenerable junk can go in one click. Personal or uncertain data stays in Review. NUKE never guesses with your files.

⌾
PRIVACY, NOT A PROMISE

Your files stay yours.

NUKE has no cloud scanner, account, analytics SDK or telemetry. File analysis happens on your Mac. Full Disk Access is optional and only unlocks a deeper scan of macOS-protected locations.
